About

A Voice for the Wild

Roger Liu

High School Student · Wildlife Enthusiast

I am a high school student with a deep love for wildlife. From watching documentaries about endangered species as a child to leading my school's biology club today, the natural world has always been my deepest concern. Wildlife conservation, biodiversity, green development — these are not just academic terms, but the directions of my daily thought and action.

My path runs from the desk to the field and back: designing a controlled experiment on microplastics and earthworms for an independent research program, surveying species on mountain transects, counting bird-window collisions around campus, and writing down what all of it teaches me. This website is my field journal — a record of observations, thoughts, and actions.

If you too care about the future of wildlife on this planet, you are welcome to stay, read, and join the conversation.

Fieldwork & Research

2026 Buqing Research Program

Microplastics, Earthworms & Soil Water Safety

Independent research project on how microplastic pollution affects Eisenia fetida and soil water environments — designing controlled mesocosm experiments, exposure groups, and testing protocols.

2026 Nanjing Agricultural University

Biodiversity Field Practicum

Multi-day field practicum in the Huangshan region: transect surveys, species identification, and field records — learning how ecologists actually count and document life.

2025 — School Biology Club

Club Lead & Program Designer

Planning and running a weekly, 90-minute club program: a full-semester series of ten hands-on biology and chemistry experiments, balanced between fun and challenge.

2025 Community Science

Bird–Window Collision Survey

Participating in a citizen-science survey of bird-window collisions: systematic patrol routes, collision records, and proposals for bird-friendly glass treatments.

2025 Roadkill Monitoring

Roadkill Survey Workshop

Workshop on roadkill monitoring: survey protocols, species identification from remains, and how volunteer data feeds into wildlife-vehicle collision research.

2025–26 Cornell Online Programs

Conservation & Environmental Education Coursework

Online coursework and projects with Cornell programs: biodiversity conservation, urban environmental education, and a campus plastic-reduction action project.

Core Themes

Three Paths, One Wild

01

Wildlife Protection

Following the plight of endangered species and imagining coexistence between humans and wildlife. From snow leopards on the Tibetan Plateau to sea turtles in the Pacific, every species tells a story worth hearing.

02

Biodiversity

From a single wetland to an entire rainforest, understanding how the web of life is woven — and torn. Every species lost is a thread cut from the fabric of existence.

03

Green Development

Exploring how economic growth and ecological protection can cease to be enemies, and seeking paths to a sustainable future where both people and wildlife can thrive.
